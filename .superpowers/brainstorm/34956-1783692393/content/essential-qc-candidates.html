Which validity/QC figure earns space in the article?

Each candidate is judged by whether the visual reveals evidence that a table or sentence would conceal. The scientific recommendation is A.

A · Scanner-harmonic spectrum

Shows the actual narrow-band residual peaks at ~41, 61 and 82 Hz, the excluded scanner windows, and the retained gamma intervals. This visually validates the gamma estimand.

Why a plot: spectral localization, peak width, consistency, and separation from retained bands cannot be established by three numbers.

B · Temporal-specificity estimates

Compares pre-stimulus, ramp-up, and plateau-window prediction with confidence intervals.

Why not next: genuinely plot-worthy, but it belongs with the eventual predictive-results figure and should use final model outputs rather than the target-validity supplement.

C · Retained-trial coverage matrix

Shows whether missing trials cluster by participant or temperature.

Why not next: useful only if retention is strongly patterned; otherwise the cohort table and dose-response participant trajectories already communicate coverage.

Recommendation: A. It directly defends the most vulnerable measurement choice in Study1—the scanner-clean gamma definition—using outcome-blind evidence from every final-clean run.
